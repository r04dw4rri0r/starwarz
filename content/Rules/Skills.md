### Force Skills

The following skills are available to characters with **15 Force Ability** or above. Without formal training you get a penalty equal to the target’s **Force Resistance**.

Force skills are classified as either **(D) defense** or **(K) knowledge**.

| Name                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Force Guidance (D)**  | You can roll Force Ability as a saving throw to a failed Dexterity roll once per turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Leap (D)**            | Propelled by the Force, you may leap up to 35 feet through the air and land on your feet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Mind Speak (K)**      | Through the Force you speak to other sentient minds when their minds are open or their defenses are down for any other reason--such as pain or a strong emotion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Move Object (K)**     | The Force surrounds all things and you are one with the Force. By reaching out with the Force you may grab hold of people or things (people may resist) and move them with a thought (FAx3ft).                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Project Emotion (K)** | The ability to infuse others with their own emotions. This can be useful for calming enraged enemies or giving courage to comrades. However you must be careful not to use it as a weapon by projecting fear or anger onto others. Some have found such a temptation too strong. If the target’s Force Resistance is higher than your Force Ability, they may roll to resist the effect. |
| **Read Mind (K)**       | You can read the thoughts of another if you have a higher Force Ability than the target’s Force Resistance. If not, then the target may roll Force Resistance to prevent mind reading. Your roll determines the clarity of the thoughts they perceive.                                                                                                                                   |

The saber can travel 25 feet in one round.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

If the target’s Force Resistance is higher than the Jedi’s Force Ability they may roll to resist the suggestion.             |
