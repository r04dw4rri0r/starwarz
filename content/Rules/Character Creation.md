
#### Stat Points:

There are four groups of stats, under four headings: **Dexterity**, **Constitution**, **Intelligence**, and **Force Sensitivity**. These headings have ratings and count as stats on their own. Under each heading are three related sub-stats. When creating a character, you get four numbers to assign to the four major stat groups:

**33, 27, 21, and 15**

Divide the numbers up among the three sub-stats as desired and add them to the racial base of the stat. The max that can be distributed to a sub-stat is 12.

The header stats are equal to the chosen number divided by three plus the racial base of the stat. You can use sub-stat points to improve the header stat; adding +1 to the header stat costs 2 points, adding +2 costs 5 points, and adding +3 costs 9 points (adding +3 is the maximum).

#### Skills:

Each character starts with 2 free skills. Refer to the Skills sheet for a list of common skills and rules.

For every 2 points of Intelligence above 10, you get one additional language skill.

#### Contacts:

Each character starts with 1 free contact. Additional contacts may be acquired through background rolls or occupation. You can create your own free contact but you still roll to determine their status in their organization.

#### Perks:

If you have a 15 or above in any of the right-column stats, roll a 1d4 to determine which corresponding perk(s) you receive.

###### Constitution

1. Lungs of Durasteel: -2 bonus to resist airborne contaminants and toxins
    
2. Strong Stomach: tolerate disgusting meals, -2 bonus to resist ingested contaminants and toxins
    
3. Acclimated: -2 bonus to resist the effects of the climate of your homeworld
    
4. Wide Awake: -2 bonus to remain conscious
###### Strength

1. Start with Swimming skill
    
2. Start with Climbing skill
    
3. Start with Athletics skill
    
4. Start with Acrobatics skill
###### Fortitude

1. Thick-Blooded: some wounds close automatically, -2 bonus to staunch own wounds
    
2. Pain Tolerant: -2 bonus to resist pain
    
3. Sturdy: -2 bonus to resist concussive damage including from falls
    
4. Workhorse: can work longer without resting
###### Senses

1. Keen Eyes: -2 bonus to seeing subtle movement
    
2. Sensitive Hearing: -2 bonus to hearing subtle sounds
    
3. On-Guard: -2 bonus to notice when someone, including a droid, is behaving strangely
    
4. Metronomic Mind: can keep time in their own head
###### Force Sense

1. Sense Danger: roll before something dangerous is about to happen to get an instinctive feeling
    
2. Sense Emotions: roll to notice a sudden change in emotions or someone with strong emotions nearby
    
3. Sense Destiny: roll before a simple decision may have significant ramifications
    
4. Sense Force: automatically sense when force users are nearby
###### Force Ability

1. Start with Move Object
    
2. Start with Read Mind
    
3. Start with Project Emotion
    
4. Start with Mind Speak
###### Force Resistance

1. Blend in: -2 bonus to remaining hidden from other force users
    
2. Unshakable Mind: -2 bonus to resist mental/emotion force abilities
    
3. Unshakable Body: -2 bonus to resist physical force abilities
    
4. Force Deflection: when you see someone using the force on someone else, you can roll to disrupt or redirect their force ability as a reaction
###### Luck

1. Beauty -2 bonus to opposing sex
    
2. Nobility -2 bonus in official settings
    
3. Inheritance (bonus starting credits and small monthly stipend roll)
    
4. Beginner’s Luck; reduce the penalty to do something you’re not trained in

#### Occupations

##### Bounty Hunter
- **+1 Blaster, +2 Slicing, +1 Charisma**
- **Cartography and Xenology skills**
- Start with a legal weapon with a permit
- Can waive certain restrictions on laws in the act of apprehending a wanted criminal
- May have limited access to most security systems with proof of contract
- Races with Hunter Sense start with 1000 additional credits; 1500 if combined with Hunting skill
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d6 Fame roll
##### Doctor
- **+1 Constitution, +1 Intelligence, +1 Slicing**
- **First Aid skill**
- Start with a medical kit
- Start with 500 additional credits
- 1d4 Fame roll
- Choose a Primary (x2) and a Secondary (x1) medical skill
	- Cyber Implantation 
	- Major Surgery
	- Xenology 
	- Cosmetic Surgery
	- (Species) Biology
	- Psychology/Xeno-Psychology
##### Jedi Knight
- Start with a lightsaber (any color)
- Can sense Force-strong beings and strong emotions or danger
- 1d6 Fame roll
- Choose three Knowledge and three Defense skills:
	- Knowledge
		- Healing
		- Jedi Trance
		- Mind Speak
		- Move Object
		- Project Emotion
		- Read Mind
		- Suggestion
		- Battlemind
		- Force Concealment
	- Defense
		- Deflect
		- Force Guidance
		- Force Hit
		- Ignore Pain
		- Infuse
		- Leap
		- Saber Hurl
		- Stun
		- Force Barrier

##### Mechanic
- **+1 Dexterity, +3 Tech, +1 Slicing, +1 Senses**
- **Repair skill**
- Start with a repair kit of your choice
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d4 Fame roll
- Choose a Primary (x2) and a Secondary (x1) tech skill
	- Blaster Modification 
	- Droid Assembly 
	- Small Craft Tech
	- Starship Engines 
	- Starship Modification 
	- Cybernetics
	- Armor Smithing 
	- Shield Tech (all kinds)

- **+1 Dexterity, +2 Blaster, +1 Melee**
- **Blaster skill (optional specialization)**
- Start with a legal weapon
- Start with one combat maneuver
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d5 Fame roll

- Start with a legal weapon
- Start with one combat maneuver
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d5 Fame roll

##### Mercenary Pilot
- **+1 Blaster, +1 Tech, +2 Piloting**
- Start with one combat maneuver
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d5 Fame roll

##### Privateer
- **+1 Piloting, +1 Tech**
- **Piloting and Ship Repair skills**
- Start with a small, empty ship (hyperdrive and Mark I computer)
- 1d5 Fame roll

##### Slicer
- **+3 Slicing, +3 Tech**
- **Knowledge of Security Systems skill**
- Start with a computer pad with scomp link
- Start with one additional random contact
- 1d4 Fame roll
- Choose a Primary (x2) and a Secondary (x1) slicing skill
	- AI Programming 
	- Alien Systems 
	- Codebreaking
	- Data Retrieval 
	- File Alteration 
	- Program Creation
	- Security Systems 
	- Starship Computers

##### Smuggler
- **+1 Blaster, +2 Charisma**
- **Piloting, Cartography, Knowledge of Illegal Goods and Knowledge of Law skills**
- Start with a position on a ship (unless you own one)
- Not available to Mon Calamari
- Start with two additional random contacts
- 1d6 Fame roll

##### Explorer
- **+1 Pilot, +1 Int**
- **Cartography Skill**
- **Mineral Mining or Xenology or Archaeology or a Biological science skill**
- Start with a small unarmed ship with 1 piece of commercial equipment worth 5000c or less, and otherwise empty (as Privateer). Can be: 
	- *Imperial Academy of Explorers*: +1 Imp contact, can get imperial contracts, Imp services when needed (1 free), occasional non-optional missions
	- *Explorers Guild*: +1 guild contact, can get guild contracts, guild services when needed (fees apply)
	- *Independent*: +1 Fixer contact, +2 tech

##### Fame roll
- 1 - Insignificant - Once in a while you get calls but you usually seek out jobs
- 2 - Known - You can’t quite live off of calls you are offered and still have to seek them out
- 3 - Professional - You could live off of the jobs you get form word-of-mouth
- 4 - Famous - Sometimes you have to refuse one job for another
- 5 - Infamous - You should watch your back and beware of setups
- 6 - Galactic Fame - Never accept a job without checking it out first. Get one additional History roll
